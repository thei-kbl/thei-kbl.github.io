About the lab

Researching everything audio.

KBLsystems is the working notebook of Thei Klein: part sound engineering practice, part measurement bench, part stubborn search for what actually happens between a signal and an ear.

My name is Thei. Welcome to my little spot on the internetz. I have been working many many years as a sound engineer and Sound&Light contractor in (sub-) cultural live music. I nowadays concentrate on researching that miraculous construct we call Audio Reproduction.

Discovering and employing everything there is to know. Trying to work from a firm scientific background gathering all necessary knowledge and to redistribute in a hopefully comprehensive way without mentioning to much mathematics.

Enjoy reading along!
