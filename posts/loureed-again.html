LouReed rises again..

Mar 2014Past Projects1 min readBy Thei Munten

On the bench are two battered LouReed cabinets that spent a year and a half in the woods after being stolen from a van. The corners are ruined; the 12-inch drivers, astonishingly, are not.

More than a year and a half ago, one of our LouReed sound systems was stolen from a parked van. Surprise, surprise: an attentive civilian recently found this in the woods.

Shock and horror: our meticulously crafted cabinets, tossed away. Probably not useful to the people who stole them, as they need a separate FIR-filtered crossover to get anything like a decent sound.

Today I have been refurbishing them, and to my really big astonishment the 12-inch speakers are still full-spec functional. Hooray for Mr Simon Leung (A&D Audio) for the excellent manufacturing quality. The 1-inch drivers were less fortunate — not that they would be of lesser build quality, but of course these are a lot more vulnerable.

So here comes LouReed Phoenix. While we are at it, we will also update the FIR filtering for these cabinets. For those interested in FIR: Thomas Drugeon (aka pos) made an excellent utility, rePhase.

And, oh yes, I also made a quad amplifier using Hypex UCD400 blocks and a switch-mode PSU. If we could now find some decent DSP modules (with FIR!), we could finally make our powered cabinets. (Nah, sorry, no miniDSP: nice, but not stable enough for our "pro-audio abuse".) I might have another idea though; will get back to you.
